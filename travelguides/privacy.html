TravelGuides Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Who we are

TravelGuides (JamminTravelGuides on the App Store) is made by Fact Forge LLC ("Fact Forge," "we," "our" or "us"). TravelGuides reads travel guides day by day, with routes, stops and maps, and lets you build your own. This policy explains what TravelGuides collects, how we use it, who we share it with and how long we keep it.

What we collect

  • Nothing is sent to us. TravelGuides has no account, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • On your phone only. Your guides and trip details (flights, bookings, emergency contact and notes) stay on your phone. They aren't synced, and they aren't included when you share a guide.
  • Location. If you allow it, the map shows where you are on the route. Your location is read on your phone only. It isn't stored or sent anywhere.

How we use it

  • The app uses your guides and trip details to show your trip, and your location to put you on the map, on your phone.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data. TravelGuides shows no ads, has no advertising or ad-tracking code, and doesn't ask to track you.

Who we share it with

  • Apple distributes the app and provides the maps (Apple Maps). Apple's privacy policy
  • Frankfurter, a free exchange-rate service, provides the exchange rates the app shows. Each request names only the two currencies. Like any website, Frankfurter receives your IP address. frankfurter.dev
  • When you share a guide, it goes only where you send it.

How long we keep it and how to delete it

  • Your guides and trip details stay on your phone until you delete them or the app. Your phone's own backup can include them.

Children

TravelGuides is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: factforge@atomicmail.io.
