RunSome Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Who we are

RunSome is made by Fact Forge LLC ("Fact Forge," "we," "our" or "us"). RunSome is a running game. It records your runs with GPS, turns the ground you cover into territory on a shared map, and holds events with prizes. This policy explains what RunSome collects, how we use it, who we share it with and how long we keep it.

What we collect

  • Account. You sign in with your Google account. Firebase Authentication stores your basic Google profile: your name, email address and a link to your profile photo. Our server stores your name and email address with the account ID that Firebase assigns.
  • Location and runs. While you record a run, the app reads your precise location, in the background too, to draw your route. When you finish, it uploads the run to our server: the route (each point's position, height and time) and the run's name, notes and type. Starting with the next version, a run that can't upload yet waits on your phone until it can.
  • Territory. Our server turns your routes into territory and ranks you in events.
  • Strava. If you connect Strava, our server stores your Strava athlete ID and the access keys Strava issues, and the app keeps those keys on your phone.
  • Apple Health. The next version can import your past runs. If you choose to, it reads your workouts and their routes from Apple Health and uploads them as runs. RunSome never writes to Health. The current test version doesn't use Health.
  • Crash reports and usage data. Firebase Crashlytics sends us crash reports and error details, such as device model, iOS version, app version and where in the app the problem happened. Firebase Analytics records how the app is used: app launches, which tab you open, which event you open (by the event's name), sign-ins and sign-outs, account deletion, sharing the app, and GPS problems such as low accuracy. These events come with Firebase's app-instance ID and basic device information, and Google works out your general location, such as your city or country, from your IP address. They never include your routes or your name, and they are not linked to your account.
  • On your phone only. During a run, your time and distance show on your Lock Screen. This happens on your phone.

How we use it

  • To record your runs, build territory, run events and leaderboards, and keep you signed in.
  • To post your runs to Strava, if you connect it.
  • To find and fix problems, through crash reports, and to see how the app is used, through analytics.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data. RunSome shows no ads, has no advertising or ad-tracking code, and doesn't ask to track you.

Who we share it with

  • Other players. Other players see your territory on the map and your rank on event leaderboards, with your name. Because territory follows your routes, other players can see the areas where you run.
  • Strava receives each run, if you connect it. Strava's privacy policy
  • Google Firebase provides sign-in (Firebase Authentication), crash reporting (Crashlytics) and analytics (Firebase Analytics). Firebase encrypts data in transit with HTTPS. Firebase Authentication runs only in US data centers. Google may store and process other data wherever it has facilities. Firebase privacy and security
  • Google provides Google sign-in. Google Privacy Policy
  • Amazon Web Services hosts our server. AWS privacy notice
  • OpenFreeMap and CARTO provide the map images. Like any website, they receive your IP address and see which part of the map you're viewing. OpenFreeMap privacy, CARTO privacy
  • Apple distributes the app. Apple's privacy policy

How long we keep it and how to delete it

  • We keep your account, runs and territory until your account is deleted.
  • In the current test version (0.1.0, build 18), Delete account removes your sign-in account but not your runs on our server. Email factforge@atomicmail.io and we will delete them.
  • In the next version, Delete account also deletes your account from our server and disconnects RunSome from Strava. Your runs and territory stay in the game, moved to one shared anonymous account, and are no longer linked to you.
  • Disconnecting Strava in the app stops the posts to Strava.
  • Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. Firebase Authentication keeps the IP addresses it logs for a few weeks.

Children

RunSome is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
