All in One Notes Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Who we are

All in One Notes (AON on your Home Screen) is made by Fact Forge LLC ("Fact Forge," "we," "our" or "us"). It keeps your to-dos, lists and recurring tasks, with your calendar alongside. This policy explains what All in One Notes collects, how we use it, who we share it with and how long we keep it.

What we collect

  • On your device first. Notes, lists, recurring tasks, task details and your completion history are stored on your device. If you don't sign in, none of it leaves the device.
  • Account. Signing in is optional. You can sign in with Apple or Google. Firebase Authentication then stores your account ID and the email address and name that Apple or Google share.
  • Synced data. While you're signed in, the app syncs your notes, lists, recurring tasks, task details (due dates, notes and steps) and completion history (what was completed and when) to our cloud database, under your account. Other users can't read them.
  • Your devices. While you're signed in, each device records its name, type (iPhone, iPad or Mac), app version and when it was last seen, so the Sync page can list your devices. On a Mac, the name is the computer's name.
  • Calendar. If you allow it, the app reads your calendar events to show them with your to-dos. If you turn on recurring-task sync, it adds events to a calendar you choose. Your calendar events are never sent to us.
  • Crash reports and usage data. Firebase Crashlytics sends us crash reports and error details, such as device model, operating system version, app version and where in the app the problem happened. Firebase Analytics records how the app is used: screens opened, setup steps, sign-ins and sign-outs, notes added, completed, moved or deleted, lists and recurring tasks added or changed, calendar events created or deleted, searches (by number of results), how long syncing takes, which settings you change, errors by kind and your answer to the calendar permission. These events come with Firebase's app-instance ID and basic device information, and Google works out your general location, such as your city or country, from your IP address. They never include note text, list or task names, event titles, your name or your email address, and they are not linked to your account.
  • On a Mac. The app answers requests from other programs on the same Mac, so they can read and change your notes. Nothing outside the Mac can reach it, and you can turn it off in Settings.

How we use it

  • To keep your notes on your devices and, if you sign in, in sync between them.
  • To show your calendar with your to-dos, and to add recurring tasks to your calendar if you ask.
  • To find and fix problems, through crash reports, and to see how the app is used, through analytics.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data. All in One Notes shows no ads, has no advertising or ad-tracking code, and doesn't ask to track you.

Who we share it with

  • Google Firebase provides sign-in (Firebase Authentication), our cloud database (Cloud Firestore), crash reporting (Crashlytics) and analytics (Firebase Analytics). Firebase encrypts data in transit with HTTPS. Firebase Authentication runs only in US data centers. Google may store and process other data wherever it has facilities. Firebase privacy and security
  • Google provides Google sign-in. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple distributes the app and provides Sign in with Apple. If you use Siri with the app, Apple's Siri handles what you say, and the app receives the text. Apple's privacy policy

How long we keep it and how to delete it

  • We keep synced data until you delete it. Deleting a note, list, recurring task or task's details also deletes it from our cloud database. The record of when things were completed stays.
  • Signing out stops syncing. Your notes stay on the device.
  • The app can't delete your account yet. Email factforge@atomicmail.io and we will delete your account and everything synced to it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. Firebase Authentication keeps the IP addresses it logs for a few weeks.

Children

All in One Notes is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
