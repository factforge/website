DayOf Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Who we are

DayOf is made by Fact Forge LLC ("Fact Forge," "we," "our" or "us"). DayOf plans a wedding day and gives everyone in it their own schedule on their phone. This policy explains what DayOf collects, how we use it, who we share it with and how long we keep it.

What we collect

  • Your plan. The couple's names, the wedding dates and time zone, the guest count, the wedding website you enter, the places, the schedule and its notes, the people in it and the updates you send. DayOf stores the plan on your phone and in our cloud database.
  • People in the plan. For each person: their name, group and role, and, if you add them, their email address and phone number. You can fill these in from your contacts. The app reads only the contact you pick.
  • Account. DayOf signs you in with an anonymous account from Firebase Authentication, without asking for anything. You can link Sign in with Apple or Google to open your plan on another phone. Firebase then stores the email address and name that Apple or Google share.
  • Crash reports and usage data. Starting with the next version, Firebase Crashlytics sends us crash reports, and Firebase Analytics records how the app is used: screens opened, setup steps, changes to the plan, its people and places (as counts and kinds), sharing and joining, updates sent, Live Activities, sync status, slow steps and errors by kind. These events come with Firebase's app-instance ID and basic device information, and Google works out your general location, such as your city or country, from your IP address. They never include names, email addresses, phone numbers, anything you type, places, dates or IDs. The current test version (0.1, build 1) sends no analytics or crash reports.
  • On your phone only. The camera is used only to scan an invite's QR code, on your phone. On the day, what's happening now can show on your Lock Screen. This happens on your phone too.
  • Purchases. DayOf doesn't sell anything yet. It includes RevenueCat's purchase code for a planned one-time unlock, but it's switched off and never contacts RevenueCat. We will update this policy before that changes.

How we use it

  • To keep your plan in sync between your phones and to give the people in it their schedules.
  • To find and fix problems, through crash reports, and to see how the app is used, through analytics.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data. DayOf shows no ads, has no advertising or ad-tracking code, and doesn't ask to track you.

Who we share it with

  • The people in your plan. Sharing makes invite links. Anyone with a person's link can open that person's schedule. Anyone with a group's link can open the schedule of anyone in the group. A schedule shows everyone's names and roles, but only the email address and phone number of the person it's for and of their day-of contact. People who open a link get an anonymous account too, and their phone keeps a copy of their schedule.
  • Google Firebase provides sign-in (Firebase Authentication) and our cloud database (Cloud Firestore), and, starting with the next version, crash reporting (Crashlytics) and analytics (Firebase Analytics). Firebase encrypts data in transit with HTTPS. Firebase Authentication runs only in US data centers. Google may store and process other data wherever it has facilities. Firebase privacy and security
  • Google provides Google sign-in. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple distributes the app and provides Sign in with Apple. Venue search and driving times use Apple Maps, which receives what you type and the places you choose. Apple's privacy policy
  • RevenueCat is built into the app for a planned purchase and isn't used yet. RevenueCat's privacy policy

How long we keep it and how to delete it

  • We keep the plan until it is deleted. Delete wedding, in Settings, deletes the plan, its people, updates, schedules and invite links from your phone and from our servers. If you joined someone else's plan, deleting it on your phone takes you out of that plan.
  • Turning off or replacing an invite link stops it from working, but phones that already joined stay joined.
  • Deleting a wedding doesn't delete your sign-in account, and the app can't delete accounts yet. Email factforge@atomicmail.io and we will delete yours.
  • Firebase Authentication keeps the IP addresses it logs for a few weeks. Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

Children

DayOf is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
