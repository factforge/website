Red White and Blue Book Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Who we are

Red White and Blue Book is made by Fact Forge LLC ("Fact Forge," "we," "our" or "us"). It shows a verse from the King James Bible each day. This policy explains what Red White and Blue Book collects, how we use it, who we share it with and how long we keep it.

What we collect

  • Usage data. Firebase Analytics records how the app is used: which verses you view and share (by reference, such as John 3:16), swipes, opening settings, and the font and app icon you pick. These events come with Firebase's app-instance ID and basic device information, and Google works out your general location, such as your city or country, from your IP address.
  • On your phone only. The app has no account, and the verses are built in. Your settings stay on your phone.
  • What it doesn't collect. It has no crash reporting, and it doesn't use your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

How we use it

  • To see how the app is used, through analytics.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data. Red White and Blue Book shows no ads, has no advertising or ad-tracking code, and doesn't ask to track you.

Who we share it with

  • Google Firebase provides analytics (Firebase Analytics). Firebase encrypts data in transit with HTTPS. Google may store and process this data wherever it has facilities. Firebase privacy and security
  • Apple distributes the app. Apple's privacy policy
  • A verse you share goes only where you send it.

How long we keep it and how to delete it

  • Your settings stay on your phone until you delete the app, which deletes them.

Children

Red White and Blue Book is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact
