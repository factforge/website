About

Fact Forge LLC is an app development studio focused on building well-crafted applications. We design and develop software from the ground up, with close attention to user experience, performance, and privacy.

We are committed to getting information out to our users in an easily digestible way that you'll be comfortable with. No dark patterns, no data harvesting, no overwhelming interfaces — just clear, honest software that respects your time and attention.

Every app we ship is something we would want to use ourselves. If we wouldn't trust it with our own data or recommend it to someone we know, it doesn't leave our studio.

FAQ

What does Fact Forge do?

We design and develop applications. Our focus is on building polished, privacy-respecting apps that solve real problems.

Is my data private?

It depends on the app. Each app's privacy policy says what it collects, why, and how to delete it. See our privacy policies.

How can I get support for an app?

Visit our support page or email us at factforge@atomicmail.io. Include your device model, iOS version, and a description of the issue.

Can I request a feature?

Absolutely. Send your ideas to factforge@atomicmail.io and we'll take a look.
