TaxTab Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Who we are

TaxTab (TaxesTabbed on the App Store) is made by Fact Forge LLC ("Fact Forge," "we," "our" or "us"). TaxTab shows your income taxes as a receipt: what you pay to federal, state and local governments, and what the money is spent on. This policy explains what TaxTab collects, how we use it, who we share it with and how long we keep it.

What we collect

  • Nothing. TaxTab works entirely on your phone. It has no account, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it sends nothing to us or anyone else.
  • On your phone only. Your income, filing status, state and locality are stored only on your phone.

How we use it

  • The app uses what you enter to work out your receipt, on your phone.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data. TaxTab shows no ads, has no advertising or ad-tracking code, and doesn't ask to track you.

Who we share it with

  • No one. If you share your receipt, it goes only where you send it.
  • Apple distributes the app. Apple's privacy policy

How long we keep it and how to delete it

  • What you enter stays on your phone until you delete the app, which deletes it. Your phone's own backup can include it.

Children

TaxTab is not made for children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: factforge@atomicmail.io.
