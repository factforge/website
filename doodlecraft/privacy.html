Doodle Craft Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

The short version

Doodle Craft is a game for kids made by Fact Forge LLC ("we," "our," or "us"). It doesn't collect any information about you or your child, and nothing in it leaves your device.

What the game collects

Nothing. Doodle Craft doesn't collect, store or share personal information from anyone.

  • No accounts or sign-in, and nothing to type
  • No ads, and no third-party analytics or tracking
  • No purchases, no links out of the game, no sharing and no chat
  • No use of the camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location
  • The game doesn't send anything over the internet

Saved games

Progress and settings are saved only on your device, and deleting the game deletes them. If your device is backed up, for example with iCloud Backup, the save is part of that backup, which Apple handles under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Information from Apple

Apple may show us combined, anonymous numbers about the game, such as how many people downloaded it, and crash reports from people who chose to share them with developers. None of it tells us who you or your child are.

Children's privacy

Doodle Craft is made for children. Because it collects no personal information, including no device identifiers, there is nothing for a parent to consent to, review or delete under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Parents can write to us with any question, and we will answer.

Changes

If we change what the game does with information, we will update this page, with a new date, before the change reaches the game, and ask for a parent's consent wherever the law requires it.
