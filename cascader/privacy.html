Cascader Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Who we are

Cascader is made by Fact Forge LLC ("Fact Forge," "we," "our" or "us"). Cascader turns recipes into a cascade, a step-by-step grid you cook from. You can add a recipe from a link, a photo, pasted text or by speaking. This policy explains what Cascader collects, how we use it, who we share it with and how long we keep it.

What we collect

  • Recipes stay on your phone. Your recipes, shopping list, settings and cooking progress are stored on your phone. Cascader has no account, and we have no server for it.
  • Recipe links. When you add a recipe from a link, the app downloads the page straight from the recipe's website. The link is saved with the recipe.
  • Photos and camera. When you take or pick a photo of a recipe, the app reads its text on your phone. The photo isn't sent anywhere.
  • Speaking a recipe. When you dictate, your speech is turned into text on your iPhone. The audio isn't kept or sent anywhere.
  • Smart import. If the usual reader finds no recipe on a page, Apple's on-device model reads it, on your iPhone.
  • Timers. Step alerts, and the step and timer shown on your Lock Screen while you cook, are set up on your phone.
  • Crash reports and usage data. Starting with the next version, Firebase Crashlytics sends us crash reports, and Firebase Analytics records how the app is used: screens opened, how you add recipes, how much the reader found and how long steps took (both as ranges), and errors by kind. These events come with Firebase's app-instance ID and basic device information, and Google works out your general location, such as your city or country, from your IP address. They never include recipe titles, ingredients, steps, links or anything you type or say. The current test version (0.1, build 5) sends no analytics or crash reports.
  • Android. The Android version also keeps your recipes on the phone. It saves recipe links without downloading the pages, and it doesn't use the camera or microphone. It sends the same crash reports and analytics, and it never collects your phone's advertising ID.

How we use it

  • To add your recipes and lay them out for cooking, on your phone.
  • To find and fix problems, through crash reports, and to see how the app is used, through analytics.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data. Cascader shows no ads, has no advertising or ad-tracking code, and doesn't ask to track you.

Who we share it with

  • Recipe websites. When you add a recipe from a link, the recipe's website receives your IP address, like any website.
  • Google Firebase provides crash reporting (Crashlytics) and analytics (Firebase Analytics), starting with the next version. Firebase encrypts data in transit with HTTPS. Google may store and process this data wherever it has facilities. Firebase privacy and security
  • Apple distributes the app. If you send your shopping list to Reminders, the app adds it to a Cascader list in Apple's Reminders app. Apple's privacy policy

How long we keep it and how to delete it

  • Delete a recipe in the app, or delete the app to remove everything. Your phone's own backup can include your recipes.
  • Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

Children

Cascader is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
