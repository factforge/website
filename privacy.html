Privacy Policy

Last updated July 20, 2026

Overview

Fact Forge LLC ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy describes how we handle information in connection with our applications and this website.

Information We Collect

Our apps are designed with privacy in mind. We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our services.

  • Usage data — We may collect anonymous, aggregated analytics to understand how our apps are used. This data cannot identify individual users.
  • Support inquiries — If you contact us, we collect the information you provide solely to respond to your request.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties
  • We do not collect personal information for advertising
  • We do not use third-party tracking or advertising SDKs
  • We do not require account creation to use our apps

Data Storage

App data is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain servers that store personal information. Data you create within our apps remains on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

Third-Party Services

Our apps may use Apple's built-in frameworks and services. These are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Our apps do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
