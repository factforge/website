Pitch Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Who we are

Pitch (PitchPlace on the App Store) is made by Fact Forge LLC ("Fact Forge," "we," "our" or "us"). Pitch is a shared feed where a team pitches app ideas, gives verdicts, talks them over and decides which to build. This policy explains what Pitch collects, how we use it, who we share it with and how long we keep it.

What we collect

  • Ideas and discussion. Each idea's title, pitch, status, verdicts and comments, and its text from before the last edit, with the display name you choose. They are stored in our cloud database.
  • Account. To see or post ideas, you sign in with Apple or Google. Firebase Authentication then stores your account ID and the email address and name that Apple or Google share. Ideas and comments you post while signed in carry your account ID. The app also keeps a copy of that name and email address on your phone.
  • Photos. The photos you add to an idea, shrunk to fit. Each one is kept on your phone and, if you're signed in, uploaded to our cloud database. Pitch asks before it saves a photo to your library.
  • Usage data. Firebase Analytics records how the app is used: when it opens, which screens are opened, and actions such as pitching, opening, editing or deleting an idea, giving a verdict, changing a status, commenting, adding photos and filtering. These events carry only counts and choices, such as a status or a verdict. They never include idea text, comments, names or email addresses. They come with Firebase's app-instance ID and basic device information, and Google works out your general location, such as your city or country, from your IP address. They are not linked to your account.
  • On your phone only. Your display name, recent searches, the ideas you've seen, snoozed decisions, decision reminders and your settings stay on your phone.
  • What Pitch doesn't collect. Pitch has no crash reporting, and it doesn't use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

How we use it

  • To run the shared feed: to show ideas, comments, verdicts and photos to the people who use Pitch, and to keep you signed in.
  • To research ideas. We use Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic, to read the ideas and comments in Pitch and research each idea, for example by searching the web for apps like it. Its reports, and the tasks it takes from the team's comments, are kept in our own files.
  • To see how the app is used, through analytics.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data. Pitch shows no ads, has no advertising or ad-tracking code, and doesn't ask to track you.

Who we share it with

  • Everyone who uses Pitch. Pitch has one shared feed. Everyone who uses it can see every idea, and can comment on it, give a verdict, change its status or delete it. Anyone signed in to Pitch can see the photos.
  • Google Firebase provides sign-in (Firebase Authentication), our cloud database (Cloud Firestore) and analytics (Firebase Analytics). Firebase encrypts data in transit with HTTPS. Firebase Authentication runs only in US data centers. Google may store and process other data wherever it has facilities. Firebase privacy and security
  • Google provides Google sign-in. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple distributes the app and provides Sign in with Apple. If you pitch an idea with Siri, Apple's Siri handles what you say. Apple's privacy policy
  • Anthropic makes Claude, which reads Pitch's ideas and comments to research them. Anthropic's privacy policy

How long we keep it and how to delete it

  • Ideas stay in the feed until someone deletes them. Deleting an idea also deletes its verdicts, comments and photos.
  • In Settings, you can delete every idea you pitched at once.
  • If you signed in with Apple, you can delete your account in the app. That deletes the ideas you pitched and their photos, your Firebase account, and the name and email address kept on your phone, and it ends Pitch's Sign in with Apple access. Comments and verdicts you left on other people's ideas stay.
  • The app can't delete an account that uses Google sign-in yet. Email factforge@atomicmail.io and we will delete it.
  • Firebase Authentication keeps the IP addresses it logs for a few weeks.
  • Photos kept on your phone are deleted with the app.

Children

Pitch is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
