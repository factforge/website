Support

Having trouble with one of our apps? We're here to help.

Get in Touch

Email factforge@atomicmail.io and we'll respond as soon as possible. When writing, include:

  • The app you need help with
  • Your device model and OS version
  • A description of the issue

Common Questions

How do I report a bug? Email factforge@atomicmail.io with what happened, what you expected, and any steps to reproduce it.

Is my data private? It depends on the app. Each app's privacy policy says what it collects, why, and how to delete it. See our privacy policies.

How do I request a feature? Send your ideas to factforge@atomicmail.io.
